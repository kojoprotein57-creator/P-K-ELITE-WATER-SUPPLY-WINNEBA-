Our products

Sachet water

500ml sealed sachet water supplied in bundles of 30. Ideal for homes, shops, offices, roadside sales and everyday use.

Bottled water

Available in 500ml and 1.5L bottles. Suitable for offices, churches, functions, schools, shops and events. Branded or unbranded options are available depending on your requirements.

18.9L bulk refill

Reusable 18.9L gallon refills for home and office water dispensers. We can also collect empty gallons when delivering your refill.

Event and bulk supply

Planning a wedding, funeral, church programme, school event or another gathering? We provide large-volume water orders and arrange delivery according to your requirements.

How it works

  1. Place your order

    We prepare your order and confirm the delivery arrangements with you.
